# References

## Finnish Evangelical Lutheran Church (EVL)

### Visual identity and graphic assets

- [Kirkon graafinen ilme — official graphic identity guide (EVL Plus)](https://evl.fi/plus/yhteiskunta-ja-kirkko/kirkon-viestinta/kirkon-graafinen-ilme/) — overview of the church's updated visual identity, including colours, logos, typography, pictograms and background patterns. The page points to the complete graphic guide and downloadable assets in the materials bank.
- [Kirkon materiaalipankki — church materials bank](https://evl.resourcespace.com/pages/collections_featured.php?parent=199) — graphic identity materials, including pictograms, logos and background patterns. The official guide says the materials bank does not require sign-in.
- [Kohti yhteistä ilmettä — project background (EVL Plus)](https://evl.fi/plus/yhteiskunta-ja-kirkko/kirkon-viestinta/kohti-yhteista-ilmetta/) — background and decisions behind the shared visual identity.

### Photographs

- [Kirkon kuvapankki — church photo bank](https://kuvapankki.evl.fi/) — photographs for church-related communications.
- [Kirkon kuvapankin käyttöehdot — photo bank terms of use (PDF)](https://kuvapankki.evl.fi/Kirkon_kuvapankin_kayttoehdot.pdf) — check the rights and attribution requirements for each photograph; downloading an image does not transfer its copyright.

## Notes for Kirkkovuosi-Kuvatin

Treat the graphic materials bank and photo bank as separate sources. Store source and asset-level rights/attribution information with imported assets, and verify the licence and permitted use of each item before including it in recommendations or exports.
